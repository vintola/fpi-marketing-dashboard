# Sample Marketing Action Plan

A prioritised, owner-assigned plan built from the sample dashboard findings for
Freedom Property Investors. P0 items unlock measurement and trust. P1 items move
efficiency. P2 items build durable advantage.

> All data is synthetic. Validate the findings with live CRM and ad-platform data
> before committing budget.

## Findings this plan responds to

1. **Measurement gap.** Media is optimised on form fills, not members. There is
   no closed loop between HubSpot outcomes and the ad platforms.
2. **Mid-funnel leak.** Masterclass attendance and speed-to-lead are the largest
   controllable losses. Both are cheaper to fix than buying more traffic.
3. **Misallocated budget.** Search has the lowest CAC and retargeting returns
   roughly 1.8x Meta prospecting's ROAS, yet both are under-funded relative
   to prospecting and YouTube.
4. **Creative fatigue.** Meta CPM is rising while CTR falls. Founder-led and data
   angles outperform testimonial and UGC in the sample.
5. **Trust gaps on site.** Live Lorem Ipsum and inconsistent review counts add
   friction to every paid click.

## Strategic bets

- **Protect the intent engine.** Search converts at the lowest CAC. Fund it to the ceiling of available high-intent demand before buying more cold reach.
- **Treat retargeting as a profit centre.** Retargeting returns roughly 1.8x Meta prospecting's ROAS on a small share of budget. Expand it with sequenced masterclass-replay and objection-handling creative.
- **Buy YouTube for quality, measure it for incrementality.** YouTube carries the highest CAC but skews to experienced investors with higher member value. Judge it on incremental members and assisted revenue, not last-click CAC.
- **Fix the middle of the funnel.** Masterclass attendance and speed-to-lead are the largest leaks. Improving them raises member volume without increasing media spend.

## Assumptions and caveats

- **Synthetic data.** Every number in this dashboard is generated for demonstration. It is calibrated to Freedom Property Investors' public positioning (masterclass-led, high-ticket advisory, three investor segments) but is not actual company data.
- **Membership value.** First-time investor $7,800; experienced investor $12,000; retiree $9,500. Blended value in the sample is roughly $9,600 per member in year one.
- **Lifetime value.** LTV is modelled at 2.2x first-year membership revenue over three years, allowing for renewals, upsells and referrals. Replace with observed cohort LTV before using LTV:CAC for real budget decisions.
- **Attribution.** Members are attributed to the paid channel of first touch. Real attribution should combine first touch, last touch and incrementality tests. Platform ROAS is not incremental ROAS.
- **Currency and period.** All figures are AUD over the 26 weeks from 27 April to 25 October 2026.
